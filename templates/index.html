📂 Instructions for Depositing Authorized External Forensic Datasets:

  1. HackTheBox Forensics (LogJammer): Place authorized LogJammer.evtx or exported CSV into data/external/htb/. The built-in EVTX and CSV adapters will immediately parse Event ID 1102 and timestomping indicators.
  2. DFRWS Forensic Challenges: Download challenge files from dfrws.org and deposit syslog or text files into data/external/dfrws/. The regex and text normalizer will process the Linux syslog headers.
  3. SILRAD (2025): Place the authorized Sysmon CSV into data/external/silrad/. As specified in the Blueprint, SILRAD serves as a supporting ransomware benchmark, not an invented second mandatory main pipeline.
  4. Software Test Fixtures: Located at data/fixtures/ and pre-packaged for instant automated regression tests.
