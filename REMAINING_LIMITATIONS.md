| Research Gap | Status in Project 218 | Honest Scientific Reality |
|---|---|---|
| **Gap 1: Manual Forensic Log Analysis** | Partially Resolved | Automated canonical parsing and TF-IDF triage significantly accelerate investigator review, but final forensic verification remains a human responsibility. |
| **Gap 2: Limited Anti-Forensic Classification** | Partially Resolved | The primary model performs binary triage (`normal` vs `suspicious`); it does not perform validated fine-grained multi-class anti-forensic taxonomy classification. |
| **Gap 3: Heuristic & Keyword Limitations** | Partially Resolved | Combining TF-IDF NLP with heuristic rules captures contextual token associations beyond simple substring matching, but novel obfuscations remain capable of evading n-gram vocabularies. |
| **Gap 4: Limited Explainability** | Resolved (Academic Scope) | SHAP TreeExplainer provides token-level attribution and caveat disclosures; however, token contributions represent statistical model correlations rather than definitive legal proof of intent. |

## 4. Legal Admissibility Disclosures

1. **Federal Rule of Evidence 901 & Daubert / Frye Standards:**
   - Cryptographic SHA-256 digests and audit logs assist forensic examiners in demonstrating evidence integrity and procedural repeatability.
   - However, software outputs do not guarantee court admissibility. Admissibility requires human expert witness testimony, verified chain-of-custody documentation, validation of forensic tools, and compliance with statutory rules of evidence.
2. **Provisional Automated Findings:**
   - All machine learning scores and rule flags generated by ForensicShield AI are investigative triage indicators, not adjudicated legal facts.
